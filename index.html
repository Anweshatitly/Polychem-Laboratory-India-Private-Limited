About Us

Nearly Two Decades of Pharma Excellence

Polychem Laboratory (India) Private Limited is a private limited pharmaceutical company based in West Bengal, India. Established on 15 March 2007, we operate in the field of drug formulation, development, and healthcare-related services.

Headquartered in Madhyamgram, Barasat (Kolkata region), we are registered under the Registrar of Companies (ROC), Kolkata, functioning as a company limited by shares with emphasis on quality, compliance, and steady business growth.
